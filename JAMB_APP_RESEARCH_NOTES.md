# Public JAMB-App Feature Review

## Sources reviewed

This exploratory review uses publicly visible app-store descriptions and screenshots. It is a product-pattern study, not an endorsement of the apps’ question provenance or a plan to copy their branding/content.

| Product | Publicly described patterns | Implications for JAMB Quest |
|---|---|---|
| MySchool JAMB CBT + WAEC Past Questions | Claims 60,000+ questions; JAMB/WAEC/NECO/general CBT simulators; offline access; year-by-year study; bookmarks; detailed explanations; AI study assistance; lesson notes and video lessons; performance analytics; novel preparation. | JAMB Quest should make question discovery and review first-class, support realistic CBT sessions, provide saved mistakes/bookmarks, and keep its provenance labels more explicit than generic “authentic” claims. |
| O3Schools JAMB CBT & AI Tutor | Claims 31,000+ authentic/likely questions; online/offline use; topic tests; AI tutor; detailed explanations; realistic CBT; video tutorials; text-to-speech; built-in calculator; performance analytics; offline dictionary; novel preparation. | JAMB Quest can differentiate with a smaller but trusted, traceable bank; habit-based daily minimums; recovery from missed days; focused Progress/Practice/Profile/About tabs; and transparent owner-provided versus model-source labels. Calculator, accessibility audio, topic tests, and a tutor can be considered as later roadmap items. |

## Initial product observations

Leading products converge on five high-value surfaces: a fast practice entry point, a realistic CBT simulator, searchable or topic/year-based question discovery, detailed post-answer explanations, and performance feedback. Offline access is a prominent promise. Their public descriptions also bundle many content formats, so JAMB Quest should keep the core study loop focused while adding only features that reinforce its 380 comeback system.

## Emerging roadmap themes

The strongest common structure is a two-layer experience: a fast daily practice surface for retention and a deliberate exam-simulation setup for subject, year, duration, and mode selection. JAMB Quest already has the first layer through its Practice tab and daily minimum system; the next product upgrade should make the second layer a focused CBT setup flow rather than adding more dashboard cards.

## Consolidated differentiated roadmap

| Priority | JAMB Quest direction | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Import the clean Markdown sources, expose a Question Desk with source/subject/topic filters, and preserve Practice/Progress/Profile/About tabs. | This directly addresses the 10,000-question target without returning to a dense single-page dashboard. |
| Next | Add a dedicated CBT Setup flow: compulsory English, three chosen subjects, Practice versus Full Test, duration presets, source/year filters, and a pre-test summary. | This matches the clearest public simulator pattern while keeping the flow focused. |
| Next | Add bookmarks, wrong-answer recovery, exam history, topic mastery, and weak-subject drills. | These are common high-value study primitives and fit the comeback loop. |
| Later | Add accessibility utilities such as a calculator and text-to-speech, plus carefully scoped lesson notes or tutor support. | These appear in leading products but should not distract from the verified question bank and daily system. |

> The differentiation should be **trust plus consistency**: every question carries source status, and every study action contributes to a small repeatable system toward 380.
