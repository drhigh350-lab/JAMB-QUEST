# JAMB Quest: Final Product Positioning

## Product identity

> **JAMB Quest is the serious JAMB preparation weapon.** It helps candidates move from “I am practising JAMB questions” to “I know exactly what is stopping me from reaching my target score, what to practise next, and how I am improving.”

JAMB Quest must not become “everything education.” Its singular job is to make JAMB preparation **real, simple, smart, and personal**: realistic CBT simulation, trusted approved questions, clear correction, evidence-led weakness diagnosis, and targeted improvement toward a learner’s score target.

The protected learner loop is:

> **Practice → Understand → Diagnose → Improve → Repeat**

Every major feature must strengthen this loop. If it does not help a learner practise, perform better in CBT, understand an error, identify a weakness, or improve toward a target score, it does not belong in the current product.

## The promise

**Do not just practise JAMB. Know what is stopping you from reaching your target score — and fix it.**

| Product principle | Required learner outcome |
|---|---|
| **Real** | The CBT experience feels familiar, controlled, and safe under exam conditions. |
| **Simple** | A candidate can understand the next useful action and begin practising in seconds. |
| **Smart** | Performance evidence identifies the actual weak topic, not a generic subject recommendation. |
| **Personal** | Daily missions, recovery drills, pace guidance, and target-score feedback respond to the learner’s own attempts. |
| **Trusted** | Only approved, answer-safe questions are playable; unclear, diagram-dependent, or answer-uncertain records remain withheld. |

## Protected P0 and P1 work

| Priority | Product capability | Current position |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Question accuracy and readable explanations | Protected release gate; unresolved or unsafe material stays out of gameplay. |
| **P0** | Realistic single-subject CBT and full 180-question UTME mock | Implemented with timing, flags, calculator, review, keyboard shortcuts, and local interruption recovery. |
| **P0** | Subject-oriented navigation and question palette in a full mock | Implemented: learners switch directly between Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, see local numbering and per-subject answered progress, and navigate from an accessible subject-specific palette. |
| **P0** | Untimed Study and focused topic practice | Implemented, including a verified 20-question subject-and-topic Study drill. |
| **P0** | Offline resilience and safe exam recovery | Implemented PWA/offline shell and exact in-progress CBT resume protection. |
| **P1** | Weakness engine, daily mission, and exact mistake recovery | Implemented; the automatic daily mission targets the weakest available topic after a four-subject diagnostic. |
| **P1** | Actionable Progress and target-score feedback | Implemented; a UTME estimate is shown only after a completed 180-question mock. |
| **P1** | Account-backed bookmarks and personalised reminder verification | Functionality exists; final signed-in, user-mediated verification remains pending. |

## Navigation and information hierarchy

The existing four-tab layout remains intentionally compact. **Practice** is the learner’s action dashboard: today’s mission, study choices, subject CBT, full mock, and topic practice. **Progress** diagnoses accuracy, pace, weak topics, recovery work, exam history, and target-score evidence. **Profile** holds account and reminder controls; **About** explains the product purpose.

No separate “Learn” ecosystem, marketplace, social area, or broad education hub will be introduced unless it directly strengthens the protected learner loop and the user explicitly reprioritises it.

## Explicit non-goals for the current product

AI tutoring, live teaching, tutor marketplaces, community/chat, video lecture ecosystems, school finder, career tools, dynamic flashcards, formula vaults, rich diagram tooling, advanced error classification, and AI explanation levels remain deferred. Topic-plus-difficulty filtering and search remain deferred until their metadata and index source are demonstrably reliable.

## Product acceptance test

A first-time JAMB candidate should be able to open the app, recognise the next useful action, start Study or CBT quickly, answer without interface confusion, understand the correction, safely resume a disrupted CBT, inspect exact mistakes, see the weakest topic, and launch the most relevant next drill. The learner’s mental effort should go into answering JAMB questions — not deciphering the product.
