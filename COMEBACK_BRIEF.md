# Comeback from Setback — Product Brief

## Mission

**Smash 380 in JAMB by building a repeatable daily study system, not by waiting for motivation.**

The supplied Atomic Habits extract provides the governing product principle: the 380 target gives direction, while the learner’s daily system creates progress. JAMB Quest should celebrate returning after a missed day, completing a small minimum study action, reviewing mistakes, and improving the process rather than treating one bad round as failure.

## Design implications

| Principle | Product expression |
|---|---|
| Systems over goals | A daily minimum, focused missions, and clear next actions sit ahead of a distant score target. |
| Never miss twice | A setback triggers a compassionate recovery action, not a broken streak screen. |
| Make progress visible | A consistency score, activity history, subject mastery, and 380 trajectory show process improvements. |
| Review creates growth | Wrong answers become a recovery queue and contribute to comeback XP when corrected. |
| Identity drives action | The learner is framed as a “380 builder” who returns, practices, and refines. |
