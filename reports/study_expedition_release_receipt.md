# Study Expedition — Game Layer Redesign Receipt

## Why the Game Changed

The former Quest Rush interaction was a short timed quiz. It has been replaced by **Study Expedition**, a board-style learning layer built after reviewing successful mobile progression loops, route-collection board-game mechanics, and serious-game research.

| Research insight | Study Expedition response |
|---|---|
| Top mobile games sustain engagement with changing goals, progression tracks, collections, and live-style objectives rather than a single score loop. [1] | A visible personal passport records subject study stamps and secured routes. The learner chooses a territory, route contract, and study tool before each expedition. |
| *Ticket to Ride* uses collection and route completion to create a simple, repeatable map-building loop. [2] | A learner builds a solo JAMB study map through 5-, 10-, or 15-question route contracts that secure map segments when accuracy and route integrity meet the threshold. |
| Serious-game evidence supports feedback, engagement, and motivation, but warns that shallow or poorly aligned activity may not improve exam readiness. [3] | The mode uses only active approved JAMB questions, gives immediate explanation-led feedback, retains every missed card as a repair card, and hands missed records to the existing correction flow. |

## Learner Experience

The learner now chooses one of four subject territories, signs a **Scout**, **Builder**, or **Mastery** route, and packs one transparent study tool. Topic Lens only reveals the exact official topic; Focus Stamp changes rewards but never answers; Recovery Pass protects one route-integrity mark but never alters correctness. There are no countdowns, random chance drops, monetisation, paid mechanics, or CBT-history changes.

## Verification

The branded board setup and live route-card screen were visually verified at mobile size. The focused Study Expedition safeguard test passed. The complete JAMB Quest suite passed with **98 test files and 239 tests**, and TypeScript and the production build passed.
