# Live Opening Typewriter Visual Review

The JAMB Quest opening was reviewed at desktop (1280×720) and phone (390×844) dimensions on 16 August 2026. The daily Atomic Habits line is presented inside the opening note as a live keyboard-style line with a visible caret, while the JAMB Quest mark, system-led copy, and **Skip Intro** control remain immediately recognisable.

| Viewport | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop 1280×720 | Pass | The daily quote line, caret, follow-up action, four-part mark, and Skip Intro control are clear and balanced. |
| Phone 390×844 | Pass | The typing surface remains within the viewport and readable; the follow-up action wraps naturally without obscuring the caret or Skip Intro control. |

The implementation also exposes a reduced-motion path: the full quote is rendered without typing delay, the caret is hidden, and the follow-up action is shown immediately.
