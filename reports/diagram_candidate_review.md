# Diagram candidate review

The deterministic audit found 48 wording-level candidates across Biology and Chemistry. The audit is a triage list, not proof that an image is required.

Questions that refer to an original labelled figure, arrow, or omitted apparatus cannot safely receive a reconstructed visual because the source diagram is not present in the available records. Those records remain subject to source review rather than receiving a fabricated replacement.

The remaining candidates are ordinary structure, process, location, or apparatus questions that are answerable from the supplied text and concise explanation. A black-and-white diagram would be optional enrichment rather than necessary instructional content. No decorative visuals were added. This preserves the quality-before-quantity rule and the requirement that every learner-facing visual be accurate, useful, and source-safe.

A future visual pass may add restrained black-and-white diagrams only when the original source figure is available or when a question is explicitly approved for a new instructional illustration with verified labels and syllabus alignment.
