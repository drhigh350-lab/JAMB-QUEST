Records 1020036–1020037 are returned to the protected hold until a corrected label-safe flower figure replaces it.

The corrected flower replacement passed review: `II` and `III` each terminate at the centre of separate anther heads, `IV` terminates at the stigma, and no organ names or answer text appear. Records 1020036–1020037 are restored with the corrected visual.
