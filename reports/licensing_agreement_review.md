# JAMB Quest Content Licensing Agreement Review

> **Working review, not formal legal advice.** A qualified Nigerian lawyer should review the final executed agreement before JAMB Quest relies on it for a large-scale content import.

## Practical conclusion

The uploaded agreement is a strong starting point for **JAMB Quest to import and display MySchoolNG’s UTME/JAMB question content** for **Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics** in the JAMB Quest mobile and web app. It expressly permits a free or paid question-bank feature, worldwide use, and a stated lifetime term. The permission is nevertheless **non-exclusive, non-transferable, and revocable for breach**; it does not transfer ownership to JAMB Quest.[1]

JAMB Quest should **not import the MySchoolNG content yet** until the agreement is signed by both parties and the corrections below are made. Once those points are resolved, the four-subject question bank can be treated as an authorised source; existing owner-supplied Lekki Headmaster material should remain separately identified in internal provenance records because it is not included in this licence.[1]

| Topic | What the agreement currently permits | Operational meaning for JAMB Quest |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed subjects | UTME/JAMB questions for English, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, described as the entire bank and all years | Import only those four MySchoolNG subject sets under this agreement. |
| Approved platform | JAMB Quest mobile/web app | The app can host and show the content to its learners, but a separate platform needs written amendment. |
| Commercial model | Inclusion in a paid or free question-bank feature | The content may appear in free study and paid app features within JAMB Quest. |
| Territory and term | Nigeria/worldwide and lifetime | The intended geographic reach is broad, but rights remain revocable on breach. |
| Ownership | Licensor retains intellectual-property ownership | Keep source/provenance records internally; do not present the licensed source as JAMB Quest-owned authorship. |
| Prohibited activity | No standalone resale, sublicensing, third-party redistribution, or use beyond scope | Do not export, sell, or provide the raw question bank to other apps, websites, or third parties. |

## Corrections to obtain before relying on it

The following items are material and should be corrected in the signed document or a short written amendment.

| Priority | Agreement issue | Recommended correction |
|---|---|---|
| High | **Clause 6 is internally unclear.** It first says JAMB Quest is not required to credit the Licensor, then gives the nonsensical form “Question bank content licensed from [JAMB QUEST].” | Replace Clause 6 with: “**No public attribution is required.** Licensee may use the Licensed Content without displaying Licensor’s brand or identifying Licensor as the source.” If attribution is desired, name **MySchoolNG**, not JAMB Quest. |
| High | Signature lines are blank. | Obtain both actual signatures, names, titles, and dates. Confirm the Licensor signatory has authority to license the stated bank. |
| High | The agreement date is 15 August 2026, while signature dates are 13 August 2026. | Make the agreement date and execution dates consistent. |
| High | The payment reference is blank. | Insert the actual payment reference and attach or retain the receipt. |
| High | The app is an installable PWA with offline capability. “Display to end users within the app” may not expressly cover cloud storage, technical processing, or device caching. | Add express permission for JAMB Quest and its technical providers to **store, host, format, index, cache, transmit, back up, and make the Licensed Content available for offline use within JAMB Quest**, solely to operate the permitted mobile/web app. |
| Medium | The agreement says “lifetime” but also says revocable for breach. | Preserve the breach remedy, but clarify that revocation takes effect only after written notice and a reasonable cure period for remediable breaches. |
| Medium | No clear warranty that MySchoolNG owns or controls the content and can grant this licence. | Add a warranty that the Licensor owns or controls the licensed content and has authority to grant the stated rights. |

## Recommended safe intake rule

Until a fully executed and corrected version is received, keep MySchoolNG question text **reference-only**. After execution, record the agreement reference **LIC-2026-067**, licensed scope, signing date, and four authorised subjects in JAMB Quest’s internal provenance records. Import only the covered subjects, preserve all question/answer integrity checks, and do not distribute raw exports or build a separate content portal with the material.[1]

## Suggested amendment language for the PWA and attribution points

> **Technical use and offline access.** The Licensee may store, host, reproduce, format, index, cache, back up, transmit, and make the Licensed Content available for offline access on end-user devices solely as technically necessary to operate the approved JAMB Quest mobile and web application. The Licensee may use reasonable cloud, hosting, analytics, security, and content-delivery service providers for that limited purpose, provided that no provider receives rights to use or distribute the Licensed Content independently.

> **Attribution.** No public attribution to the Licensor is required within the JAMB Quest platform. The Licensee may use the Licensed Content without displaying the Licensor’s brand or identifying the Licensor as the source. This provision does not transfer ownership or authorship of the Licensed Content.
