# Practice and Topic Drill Visual Review

On 24 August 2026, the local development preview was opened with the introduction skipped and the compact Practice chooser expanded. Desktop review confirmed that the primary navigation now contains only Practice, Progress, Profile, and About. The Practice surface presents Standard CBT first, then core-subject practice and optional Lekki practice, with a separate compact **Study tools** panel inside the same tab.

The Study tools panel retains independent Topic Drill, Syllabus Journey, and Game Arcade entries. Its concise copy distinguishes their purposes: Topic Drill is the official-syllabus practice route, Syllabus Journey is reading-only, and Game Arcade is an approved-question revision break. The revised daily focus remains concise and states that it stays fixed while the daily goal is in progress.

Topic Drill was then opened from its Practice-tab entry. With Biology’s **Life processes** official area selected and the specific-topic field left at **Whole official area**, the screen showed 1,010 matching approved questions across eight official topics and enabled the **Start 20-question Area Drill** action. The optional exact-topic selector remained available alongside the whole-area choice.

The final phone review confirmed the primary navigation is limited to Practice, Progress, Profile, and About. The compact Study tools panel remains under Practice and uses its own collapsed summary, preventing its three independent routes from crowding the core Standard CBT, subject-practice, and optional Lekki choices.
