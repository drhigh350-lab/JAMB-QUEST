# Public Answer-Page Sample Review — 16 August 2026

An additional sample of four public JAMB Physics 1983 question-detail pages was reviewed as a possible route to resolve part of the held all-subject PDF. The pages expose question text, options, and a labelled answer option, but they do not meet the project’s reliable answer-evidence threshold for bulk release.

| Sampled question | Page-labelled option | Reliability finding | Import decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resonance-tube frequency ratio | B | The explanation is internally inconsistent with the displayed formula notation and has conflicting discussion. | Held |
| Non-vector quantity | B | The page gives a letter but no corroborating primary key. | Held |
| Friction statement | D | Discussion identifies unresolved conceptual disagreement, even though the page labels D. | Held |
| Force attracting an object to Earth | C | Multiple page comments identify a likely answer conflict with option E. | Held |

The sampled pages are useful **discovery evidence** but not a complete, stable, question-number-aligned answer key. No all-subject PDF record was imported or altered from this review. A supplied key, an official year-specific key, or independent corroboration that resolves every matched answer remains required.
