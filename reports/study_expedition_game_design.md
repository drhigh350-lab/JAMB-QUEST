# JAMB Quest — Study Expedition Design

## Design Decision

The timed-only **Quest Rush** model will be replaced by **Study Expedition**, a solo, board-style study journey. It borrows two durable, non-monetised loops from the research: a visible route network completed through collections, as in *Ticket to Ride*, and changing goal tracks plus collectible rewards, as observed in leading mobile games. The game is not a speed test. The central question is: **which learning route should the learner build next?**

| Design layer | Study Expedition implementation | Learning purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Board and routes | Four subject territories each show multiple route contracts, with short, medium, and ambitious paths. | Gives a learner agency and a visible reason to return. |
| Contract choices | A learner chooses a calm 5-question scouting route, a 10-question builder route, or a 15-question mastery route. | Matches effort to energy instead of forcing a timer. |
| Study resources | Correct answers earn subject stamps; route completion collects map segments. Wrong answers become repair cards. | Turns accuracy into progress while keeping mistakes visible. |
| Strategic decision | Before a run, the learner chooses a subject, a contract, and one non-answer-changing study tool: Bookmark, Topic Lens, or Recovery Pass. | Creates a meaningful plan without giving false answers or leaking a key. |
| Resolution | A route completes only after its question set and any created repair cards are acknowledged. | Preserves correction and prevents a reward loop detached from learning. |
| Progression | Completed routes connect districts, earn subject collections, and unlock larger expeditions. | Supplies a long-term visible goal without purchases, chance drops, or pressure mechanics. |

## Non-Negotiable Safety Rules

Study Expedition may only draw from the active approved learner bank. It may never invent a question, answer, explanation, topic, or diagram. It records no false CBT result and does not alter normal Practice, the saved CBT flow, or exam history. It provides an explicit exit and routes every failed question to explanation and later correction.

## First Release Slice

The first release will include the Expedition Board, four subject territories, three contract lengths, a local progress passport, visible route/collection progress, a repair-card rack, and a correction hand-off to the current review flow. Its core reward is completing a connected study map—not a countdown score.
