# JAMB Quest Syllabus Topic Map — 15 August 2026

This map is derived from the four supplied JAMB syllabus PDFs. It is the authoritative parent taxonomy for incoming question tags. A question may retain a more precise learner-facing subtopic only when that subtopic can be assigned to one of these syllabus-backed areas. Unmapped or ambiguous records remain held for review rather than being released under a guessed topic.

## Release rule

The import pipeline will normalize supplied topic labels to these parent areas, preserve a precise subtopic where it is syllabus-compatible, and hold any record that cannot be mapped confidently. The concise explanation gate will separately require an accurate learner-facing explanation of no more than five readable lines; brevity must not remove the answer reason or a necessary distinction.
