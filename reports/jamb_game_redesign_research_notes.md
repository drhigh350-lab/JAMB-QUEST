# JAMB Game Redesign — Research Notes

## Reviewed Sources

| Source | Relevant finding | Design implication for JAMB Quest |
|---|---|---|
| [Sensor Tower: Worldwide top 10 mobile games, December 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-10-worldwide-mobile-games-by-revenue-and-downloads-in-december-2025) | The leading revenue games named include Last War: Survival, Whiteout Survival, Royal Match, MONOPOLY GO!, and Candy Crush Saga. The report attributes sustained momentum to live events, progression tracks, cooperative participation, seasonal goals, and collectible rewards. | Use visible progress, changing missions, meaningful choices, and non-cash collectables. Do not copy monetisation, chance purchases, or pressure loops. |
| [Frontiers in Psychology: Serious Games as a Method for Enhancing Learning Engagement](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.889975/full) | In the reported student survey, 87.9% perceived increased motivation, 90.8% increased engagement, and 86.1% increased confidence. The paper also warns that games with basic or poorly aligned questions may not translate to assessment readiness; students asked for better feedback and assessment-like difficulty. | Make progression depend on answer-safe JAMB questions, give correction-led feedback, and retain a difficulty-aligned exam practice path. The game must be a gateway to learning, not a substitute for correction or CBT. |
| [Days of Wonder: Ticket to Ride](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride/) | Players collect matching cards, claim visible routes, expand a network, and complete tickets. The publisher describes the loop as simple route building with many repeat plays. | Translate the map and route-completion loop into a solo JAMB “study expedition”: complete subject-specific challenges to connect named learning districts, choose between routes, and build a visible revision network. No dice, money, chance purchases, or player-versus-player pressure are needed. |

## Working Design Constraints

The replacement game needs a board-like map with deliberate route choices, subject-based territory or collections, short missions, strategic use of earned non-cash study tools, and recurring but non-exploitative objectives. Every incorrect answer must remain recoverable through explanation and correction. No random paid mechanics, gambling-like odds, or answer invention will be used.
