# Public CBT App Pattern Review — August 18, 2026

JAMB Quest reviewed the public feature descriptions of TestDriller and Myschool as product references only. The review does **not** copy their interface, question content, branding, or wording.

| Product | Publicly described patterns | Original JAMB Quest interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| TestDriller UTME | Offline study, exam-like topic practice, time-limited simulations, performance analysis, corrections in result history, bookmarks, calculator, and curriculum-aligned material. | Prioritise dependable offline continuity, a visible saved-CBT recovery state, correction-first exam history, and small focused launch choices before adding broader learning modules. |
| Myschool JAMB CBT | Offline question access, custom CBT settings, year/topic practice, bookmarks, calculator, exam history, performance analysis, syllabus access, and keyword search. | Make CBT continuity and exit safety non-negotiable; expand only later with useful search/year filters and a syllabus-linked learning shelf, after the core practice → correction → diagnosis loop is reliable. |

## Immediate JAMB Quest decisions

JAMB Quest will add an original **Exam Safety Net**: immediate local session writes, browser back/reload protection, and a clear leave dialog offering *Continue exam* or *Save and exit*. It will also add a brief **first-week setup rail** for profile target, first practice, direct notifications, and understanding real progress—not fake analytics.

The product remains a focused JAMB preparation weapon, not a general learning marketplace. AI tutoring, live classes, social features, and broad content sprawl remain outside the immediate work.
