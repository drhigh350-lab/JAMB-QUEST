# Standard CBT Feature Scope

The next JAMB Quest expansion prioritizes **exam control**, **returnable revision**, and **actionable performance feedback** rather than copying another app’s branding or content.

| Learner need | JAMB Quest implementation priority |
|---|---|
| Confidence before a mock | Readiness checklist, selected-subject confirmation, count and timer options |
| Control during an exam | Pause/resume, flagged questions, saved answers, navigable question grid, submit confirmation |
| Productive follow-up | Bookmarks, topic drills, revision queue, and result-to-next-step recommendations |
| Long-term improvement | Attempt comparison, timing trend, subject performance, and weak-topic recovery |

The App Store listing for Edubaloo explicitly describes a bookmark facility plus review of previous practice-exam history and strong/weak areas, reinforcing the priority of saved questions and actionable history in this release. [1]
